DayCast is the weather app you’d open in a real storm and trust. Honest local now and official NWS alerts, readable Site Doppler and National radar, fast on a normal iPhone.

RADAR
• Site Doppler — nearest WSR-88D Level III reflectivity (polar gates)
• National radar with animation and frame scrubbing
• Storm winds — storm-relative velocity on Live (Layers)
• Future radar — 12-hour scrub on the Radar tab (Yearly)

SEVERE WEATHER
• SPC Day 1 outlook, mesoscale discussions
• Nearby storm reports (optional, Settings)
• National Weather Service watches, warnings, and advisories, with
  time-sensitive notifications

SHORT TERM
• Next-hour / Next 2 Hours precipitation
• Hourly and 10-day forecasts

AI (DayCast Pro — Monthly or Yearly)
• Sky Check — chat with current conditions; photo check when you want
• Explain Radar — a plain-language read of the frame
• Morning brief — a short written summary from Today's Take

YEARLY ONLY
• Future radar
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
• Live Activity (updates when the app refreshes weather)

WHAT'S FREE
Radar, NWS alerts, SPC products, forecasts, and next-hour precipitation
are free. Monthly adds AI and unlimited saved locations. Yearly adds
Future radar, widgets, and Live Activity. AI has a daily limit.

DATA SOURCES
Open-Meteo, the National Weather Service, the Storm Prediction Center, NEXRAD
Level III via Iowa Environmental Mesonet, RainViewer, and NASA FIRMS.

DayCast is not affiliated with NOAA, the National Weather Service, or any
government agency. It is an interpretation aid, not a warning source. Always
follow official warnings and the guidance of local emergency officials.
